The rhythm

Bossa nova is carried by the nylon-string guitar, which plays bass and chords at once, over a syncopated pattern. Switch the squares on and off, press play, and watch the wave follow.

Dm7

A simplified groove for illustration, not a transcription: a four-bar loop of Dm7, G7, Cmaj7 and A7, synthesised live in your browser. The pattern of five hits in the top lane is the one usually counted 3-3-4-3-3.

Where it comes from

Rio de Janeirolate 1950s

Bossa nova took shape in the wealthy South Zone of Rio, in neighbourhoods such as Ipanema and Copacabana.

Chega de Saudade1958

Written by Antônio Carlos Jobim and Vinícius de Moraes and recorded by João Gilberto, the song is widely credited with launching the movement.

Getz/Gilbertorecorded 1963, released 1964

The album with saxophonist Stan Getz took the style around the world. It carries "The Girl from Ipanema", sung by Astrud Gilberto in English and by João in Portuguese.

Sources: Encyclopedia.com, Bossa Nova and the Wikipedia entry. Sources differ on whether the first release of "Chega de Saudade" is dated 1958 or 1959.
