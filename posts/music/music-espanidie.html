A band from Granada

Spanish indie has many centres. One of its reference points started in Granada: Los Planetas, formed in 1993. Pick an album.

Worth knowing

Granada, 1993

Los Planetas are an indie rock group from Granada, formed in 1993. Their genres are listed as indie rock and shoegaze.

Influences

The article says they were strongly influenced by Joy Division and early-period Mercury Rev.

Flamenco

Flamenco influence is noted on La leyenda del espacio (2007) and Una ópera egipcia (2010). The Flamenquin page here has the compás.

A reference point

The article calls them "a key reference point in the world of Spanish Indie", a claim that still lacks a citation there.
