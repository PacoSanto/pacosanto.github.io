How it works

Imperfection, on purpose

Lo-fi is the opposite of hi-fi: the flaws are kept, or added. Build a small loop and turn the knobs.

Chords, a beat, vinyl-style crackle and tape dullness, all synthesised in your browser. Nothing here is from a record.

Worth knowing

Low fidelity

Lo-fi describes sound in which elements usually regarded as imperfections, such as tape hiss, misplayed notes or degraded audio, are present, sometimes on purpose. It is the opposite of high fidelity.

A button on a sampler

Lofi hip-hop originated in the underground beatmaking scene of the 2000s, after the Roland SP-303 and SP-404 samplers appeared, each with a "lo-fi" effect button.

Two names

Nujabes is often called the "godfather of lofi hip-hop", through his soundtrack work for Samurai Champloo. J Dilla is often associated with its development.

A touchstone

The 2004 album Madvillainy, by MF Doom and Madlib, is regarded as a shared touchstone for the style.

YouTube

In the late 2010s "lofi hip-hop" or "chillhop" became popular among YouTube music streamers.
