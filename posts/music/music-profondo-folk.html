How it works

The capo, or one shape in many keys

Folk guitarists often play the same few chord shapes everywhere. A capo clamps across the neck and raises every string by one semitone per fret. Choose a fret and play the three shapes.

The shapes G, C and D stay the same under your fingers; the chords you hear are higher by the number of frets.

Worth knowing

What folk means

Traditional folk music has been defined as music transmitted orally, by unknown composers, on traditional instruments. It changes from generation to generation: the "folk process".

Where the word comes from

"Folklore" was coined in 1846 by the English antiquarian William Thoms. "Folk music" is a comparatively recent extension of it. The German Romantics, led by Herder, had applied "Volk" to popular music over half a century earlier.

The revival

A second folk revival reached its zenith in the 1960s and created contemporary folk music: newly composed songs by known authors. The Grammy Awards added a Folk category in 1959.

Dylan

Bob Dylan’s "Blowin’ in the Wind" dates to 1963; "Like a Rolling Stone" was released in July 1965.
