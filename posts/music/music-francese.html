A short map

The song, in French

"Chanson" means song. In France it also names a tradition with its own rules: the words matter, and the music follows the language. A few stops on the map.

Worth knowing

The singers of the réaliste

Damia, Fréhel and Édith Piaf are named among its key performers.

Rhythm of the language

What sets chanson française apart is that it follows the rhythms of French rather than English, and expects more from the lyrics.

Across the border

The French chansonniers influenced the Italian cantautori. See the Italian playlists on this site.
