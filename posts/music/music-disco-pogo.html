How it works

1, 2, 3, 4!

Tap along to any song with Space or the button and see how fast you are jumping. Then try the count-in.

The Ramones’ early songs were "very fast and very short; most clocked in at under two minutes". The source gives no tempo figure, so I give none.

Worth knowing

The pogo

Dancers jump up and down, in place or moving around, named for the resemblance to a pogo stick. A common version keeps the torso stiff, arms rigid and legs together.

Who invented it

Sid Vicious claimed he invented the pogo around 1976 at early London punk shows. Whether he did or not, it became closely tied to punk. The source calls it a precursor to moshing.

The Ramones

Formed in 1974 in Forest Hills, Queens, New York. Their debut album was recorded in February 1976 and released on 23 April 1976: 14 songs, the longest barely over two and a half minutes.

The count-in

Dee Dee Ramone counted off each song’s tempo with a rapid-fire "1-2-3-4!".
