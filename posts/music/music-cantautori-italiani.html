A short map

Cantautore: a singer who is also the author

The word joins "cantante" and "autore". A few names and dates, only the ones I could check.

There is also a whole post on this site about one of them: Guccini (in Italian).

Worth knowing

The word

Cantautore is the Italian equivalent of singer-songwriter, from cantante (singer) and autore (author).

French roots

The French school of the chansonniers is named as an influence on the Italian cantautori.

Careful with schools

Wikipedia marks the idea of a Genoese school as needing a citation, and gives no dates for it. I have left the dates out.
