Worth knowing

Roots

Soul emerged in the late 1950s from African-American gospel and rhythm and blues, with jazz as a third element.

Call and response

The sound is built on call and response between lead and backing vocalists, an especially tense vocal sound and occasional improvised additions.

Handclaps

Catchy rhythms, stressed by handclaps and body movements. Motown’s rhythm was emphasised by handclaps or tambourine.

Memphis and Muscle Shoals

Memphis, Tennessee is where the Stax sound was nurtured. Artists such as Otis Redding and Wilson Pickett recorded at studios in Muscle Shoals, Alabama.
