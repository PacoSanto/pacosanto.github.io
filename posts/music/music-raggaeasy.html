How it works

Where the drummer leaves the first beat empty

Most pop puts the bass drum on beat one. Reggae drums often do the opposite. Pick a pattern, press play, then switch squares on and off to hear what changes.

Eight squares are one bar of four beats, counted 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &. The hi-hat row starts empty: fill it as you like.

Worth knowing

The one drop

The snare and the bass drum both sound on beat three, and beat one is left empty: the expected hit is "dropped". The snare is usually a cross-stick click.

Rockers

Essentially the one drop with a steady bass drum on every quarter note. It is often slower than the one drop.

Steppers

The one drop with a steady bass drum on every eighth note.

Who made it

Carlton Barrett, the drummer of Bob Marley and the Wailers, is credited with popularising the one drop. Who created it is disputed: his brother Aston and Winston Grennan are also named.
